VanakkamIQ logo: "Hello bubble" (finalized 2026-11-10)
A speech bubble holding a voice wave: the AI that answers your phone.

FILES (app/admin/static/brand)
  vanakkamiq-mark.svg ........ the bubble mark on its own (terracotta, cream bars)
  vanakkamiq-favicon.svg ..... browser tab icon (slightly heavier bars for 16-32 px)
  favicon.ico ................ 16 / 32 / 48 px, for older browsers
  vanakkamiq-app-icon.svg .... cream bubble on a terracotta rounded square
  apple-touch-icon-180.png ... iPhone home screen
  vanakkamiq-icon-192.png, vanakkamiq-icon-512.png ... Android / web app icons
  vanakkamiq-logo.png ........ full logo for documents and email (transparent, 1200 px wide)
  vanakkamiq-logo-dark.png ... full logo on the dark background

In the app the logo is drawn as the mark plus live text (macro `logo` in templates/shared/_ui.html),
so it stays sharp at every size. The wordmark is Manrope ExtraBold: "Vanakkam" in Ink, "IQ" in Terracotta.

COLOURS (Terracotta Sand)
  Terracotta  #A9472A   mark, "IQ", app icon background
  Cream       #FFFDFA   voice bars, mark on terracotta
  Ink         #2A1F1A   "Vanakkam" on light backgrounds
  Clay light  #E39A72   "IQ" on dark backgrounds

USE
  Keep clear space around the logo of at least half the bubble's height.
  Below 24 px, use the mark alone (no wordmark).
  Don't recolour, stretch, outline or add shadows.
